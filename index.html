What we treat

Skin, hair and nails — diagnosed properly.

Every plan begins with an actual diagnosis, not a product recommendation. Treatments are explained clearly before anything starts.

01

Acne & acne scars

Active breakouts, comedonal and hormonal acne, and post-acne marks. Medical management supported by peels and microneedling where indicated.

02

Pigmentation & melasma

Melasma, tanning, dark patches, uneven tone and hyperpigmentation, treated with prescription actives and gentle in-clinic protocols.

03

Hair fall, alopecia & PRP

Pattern hair loss, telogen effluvium, alopecia areata and dandruff. Includes scalp assessment, PRP and medically supervised regimens.

04

HydraFacial & skin brightening

HydraFacial, AquaFacial, glutathione and medi-facials — aesthetic treatments delivered under a dermatologist's supervision, not a salon's.

05

Psoriasis, eczema & skin allergies

Chronic and recurring conditions including dermatitis, psoriasis, contact allergy from cosmetics, itching and fungal infections.

06

Moles, warts & minor procedures

Mole and skin tag removal, wart treatment, keloids, stretch marks, nail disorders and skin biopsy when a diagnosis needs confirming.

Questions

Before you come in.

What does a consultation cost?

A consultation is Rs. 800 and includes a full assessment of your concern. Any procedure is quoted separately and only after you have agreed to it — nothing is added without being discussed first.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

An appointment is strongly preferred so that you are not left waiting. Message the clinic on WhatsApp using the form above and you will get the nearest available slot. Walk-ins are accommodated when there is a gap.

Where exactly is the clinic?

Pearl Classic, Ground Floor, Sane Guruji Marg, off Mahatma Gandhi Road, Motilal Nagar III, Goregaon West, Mumbai 400104 — near Azad Maidan. Tap “Directions on Google Maps” above for turn-by-turn navigation.

Are the treatments safe for Indian skin?

Yes. Every protocol is selected for medium to deep skin tones and adjusted per patient after assessment. Because Dr. Pooja is a qualified dermatologist (MD, DVL), aesthetic treatments such as HydraFacial and PRP are performed under medical supervision rather than in a salon setting.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on the condition. Acne typically improves noticeably within 6–8 weeks, pigmentation within 8–12 weeks, and hair regrowth takes around 4–6 months. You will be given realistic timelines at your first visit rather than promises.

Which days is the clinic open?

The clinic is open all seven days of the week. If you are unsure whether the doctor is in on a particular day, send a WhatsApp message and the clinic will confirm before you travel.
